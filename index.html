Summary

Your Google Business Profile is in good shape where it counts most: a 4.9-star rating from 118 reviews, 167 photos and a clear, well-written description. Customers clearly rate your work.

A handful of fixes, mainly to how the profile describes your business to Google, could help it show up for more of the right searches, such as people looking for fitted kitchens, wardrobes or a carpenter. Most are quick jobs, and none of them involve changing how you work.

We also checked your website and a few online directories against the profile. Your address and main phone number are consistent on your own website, but a few pages still say you're based in Newmarket, the "Fitted Kitchens" link in the main menu leads to a "Page not found" error, and Houzz and Golden Pages still show your old Newmarket details, phone numbers and web address.

  • A good description. It explains clearly that you're a one-stop shop for fitted kitchens and wardrobes, that all trades are organised, and that there are 7 door types and 37 styles to choose from.
  • The basics are in place. Your phone number, (022) 20289, and opening hours (Mon–Fri 9:30–17:30, Sat 10:00–17:00, Sun closed) are listed, and the website link, drummkitchens.ie, works.
  • Your website has a page for almost everything on the profile. Kitchens, wardrobes, fitted furniture, flooring, under-stairs storage, carpentry, the showroom, and media walls all have their own page.

Things we couldn't check from public view

We looked at your profile, website and directories the way a customer would, without signing in. Some things can only be checked from inside your accounts:

  • How quickly new reviews are coming in.
  • Questions and answers on the profile.
  • Attributes, such as whether you offer online estimates.
  • Whether a Services list already exists.
  • Which service areas (if any) are set on the profile.
  • Profile insights, such as how many calls and direction requests the profile gets.
  • Your Facebook page's address, phone and hours (not shown to signed-out visitors).
  • Whether the old addresses sales@drummcarpentrycork.ie and darren@drummcarpentrycork.ie still receive email.
  • Your website's sitemap, which was behind a "One moment, please…" security check when we tried it.
